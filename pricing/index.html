Pricing

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Automated Dating, a service of Peter Trai LLC, is a done-for-you dating concierge for busy professionals. Every engagement is customized, so we do not publish a fixed price list. This page explains what we offer, what it typically costs, and exactly how you will be quoted and charged. There are no surprises in this process: you will know the exact price, in writing, before you pay anything.

What we offer

There are two ways to work with us:

  • The full concierge program. Our complete done-for-you service: profile setup and optimization, professional AI-enhanced photos generated from your own photos and representing your real likeness, daily management of your dating apps by a trained human team (swiping, messaging, texting, scheduling), match approvals in your client app, and full date scheduling. Program pricing depends on duration and scope and is quoted individually on your consultation call.
  • The AI photography package on its own. Professional AI-enhanced photos without the concierge program. These packages are quoted individually based on what you need.

How pricing works

Your exact price is quoted on your consultation call, once we understand your situation, your goals, and the scope of work involved. Nothing is charged on or before that call. After the call, if we agree to work together:

  • You receive a written services agreement that itemizes the service description, the duration, and the exact price.
  • The agreement contains exactly one payment structure. There are no alternate schedules, add-on fees, or fine-print charges beyond what is written in it.
  • You sign the agreement before any charge is made. We never charge a client who has not signed.
  • All prices are in US dollars, and all charges are processed securely by Stripe.

We do not use auto-recurring billing without a signed agreement. If a future engagement (for example, a renewal) would involve any new charge, it requires a new signed agreement first.

What the price includes

For the full concierge program, the quoted price covers everything described in your agreement: photo production and approvals, ongoing daily app management by our team, match approvals delivered through your client app, and the scheduling of your dates. You will not be billed separately for messaging volume, scheduling work, or team time. If your engagement includes anything beyond the standard program, it is itemized in the agreement before you sign.

Refunds and the Date Guarantee

All payments are final and non-refundable upon signing. The sole refund remedy is our Date Guarantee: if we do not deliver the guaranteed number of dates within the ninety (90) day delivery window, your service continues free for up to sixty (60) additional days, and if dates are still undelivered after that, you receive a prorated refund of $250 per undelivered date below the guarantee. The guarantee is conditioned on reasonable client cooperation, such as timely photo approvals and responding to match approvals within 24 hours. Photos-only packages are all sales final and do not carry a date guarantee. The full terms, including conditions and how the guarantee is applied, are on our Refunds and Guarantee page, and the complete contract terms are in our Terms of Service.

Who this is for

Clients must be 18 or older. The service exists solely for genuine, lawful romantic and relationship purposes, and we manage your own dating-app profiles only with your explicit authorization. If that describes what you are looking for, we would be glad to talk.
