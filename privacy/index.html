Last updated: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Automated Dating ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit automated.dating, submit an application, book a call, or use our services.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: name, email address, phone number, and answers you submit through our application forms or during calls.

How we use it

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate (payment processing, scheduling, messaging, analytics, and advertising measurement), or when required by law. Service providers are bound to use your information only to provide services to us.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your data, email peter@automated.dating with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm once your information has been removed.

Security
